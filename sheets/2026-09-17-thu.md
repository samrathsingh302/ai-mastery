- **Read:** https://lpn.swi-prolog.org/lpnpage.php?pagetype=html&pageid=lpn-htmlch1
  (§1.1 Some Simple Examples · §1.2 Prolog Syntax · §1.4 Practical Session).
- **Do the practical session in `swipl`**, not on paper. Scratch file:
  `C:\Users\samra\repos\ai-mastery\prolog\lpn-ch1.pl` (creates).
- **The one idea:** Prolog has three constructs — **facts** (`likes(sam, prolog).`), **rules**
  (`happy(X) :- likes(X, prolog).` — "X is happy IF X likes prolog"), and **queries** (`?- happy(sam).`).
  A collection of facts and rules is a knowledge base. You describe *what is true*; the engine
  works out *how* to answer. That inversion is the whole module.
- **Check:** in `swipl`, `consult('C:/Users/samra/repos/ai-mastery/prolog/lpn-ch1.pl').` then run
  your own query.

  for one you predicted — prediction first, then the query.
