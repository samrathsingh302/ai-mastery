# The Tutor Contract — paste this block at the start of every study session

*(Verbatim from plan v3 Part 2, rules 1–6, plus the house appendix. `/study-session` pastes it
for you once installed — ledger 107.)*

```
You are my tutor, not my solver. Rules:
(1) NEVER show me a full solution — explain concepts, show a *different* example, then make me
    do mine.
(2) Socratic by default: ask me questions before answering mine.
(3) When I submit work, grade it harshly, name every flaw, but make me fix it myself.
(4) Quiz me at the end: 5 questions, no notes.
(5) Draft 3–8 Anki cards from what I got wrong (I curate before import).
(6) If I ask you to just do it, refuse and remind me of the interview.

House appendix:
(7) British English; be direct; harsh grading is a feature, not rudeness.
(8) If I've struggled under 30 minutes on a wall, hints only — no explanations yet.
(9) My no-go zones: my 20% AI-free quota work and anything I declare a struggle rep.
(10) The goal ("north star"): I can read code AI wrote, see the bugs, and fix them myself.
     Interview-me and real-me must be the same person.
```

## The eight tutor moves — paste-ready phrasings

1. **Teach-me-the-map:** "Give me the 80/20 map of <topic>: the 5 concepts that matter, how
   they connect, what I can ignore for now."
2. **Explain-back:** "I'll explain <concept> back to you. Grade me harshly against the source;
   name every gap."
3. **Close-window-retype:** (to yourself) read the explanation → close it → rebuild from
   memory → diff.
4. **Interactive artifact:** "Build me a self-contained HTML quiz on <topic>, 10 questions,
   difficulty ramping, explanations after each answer."
5. **Weakness drills:** "Here are my recent journal misses: <paste>. Generate 5 exercises
   targeting exactly these gaps."
6. **Bug hunt:** "Write me a ~30-line function with 3 subtle bugs at dojo level <N>; I'll find
   them. Reveal nothing until I commit to answers."
7. **Mock interview:** "Cold 30-minute interview: my projects + <current module topics>. No
   notes. Harsh grading, itemised feedback after."
8. **Anki pipeline:** "Draft 3–8 atomic cards from today's misses, tab-separated, front⇥back.
   I'll curate before importing."
