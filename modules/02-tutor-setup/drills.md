# Drills — module 02 (the system itself; import AFTER module 01's deck)

## Anki-importable block (tab-separated: Front ⇥ Back)

The illusion of knowing is…	Feeling you could do what you only watched being done — AI's default failure mode for learners.
The single best-evidenced study technique the contract enforces?	Active recall — answering from memory (quizzes, explain-backs) instead of re-reading.
Spaced repetition in one sentence?	Review just before you'd forget, at stretching intervals — each success pushes the next review further out.
What does FSRS do?	Learns YOUR forgetting curve and schedules each card's next review — no hand-tuning.
Your three fixed Anki numbers?	Desired retention 0.90 · 10 new cards/day · emergency backlog cap 20 reviews + bulk-suspend.
The Anki pipeline law?	AI drafts, YOU curate, only then import — unreviewed AI decks kill retention and the deck.
The wall rule?	30+ minutes of genuine struggle before Claude may even hint — walls are the curriculum.
The dojo, in one sentence?	20 daily minutes hunting planted bugs with the root-cause method — the interview muscle, built in reps.
Dojo level-up rule?	Three clean days (all bugs found in time) → level up; a brutal day means stay, never drop.
The three L0 bug classes from ex01 that recur in real code?	Swallowed error branches · unverified side-effects before destructive ops · wrong filter predicate.
The restart ritual after a broken streak?	ONE 20-minute dojo — never restart with the hardest item; never two zero-days in a row.
Why does the journal only track numbers and instruments?	Feelings don't grade — sims, scores and tallies do (imposter panic is answered with data).
The weekly outage test question?	"If Anthropic went down for a week, what % of my productivity survives?" — the number must trend up.
When is Claude banned entirely?	Struggle reps, the 20% AI-free quota, and the first 30 minutes of any wall.
Tutor contract rule 1?	Never show a full solution — concepts + a DIFFERENT example, then I do mine.
What makes 20 min/day beat 2h/week for the dojo?	Spacing applies to skills: short daily reps under mild pressure build pattern recognition.
