### Active recall
**Like you're 5:** Close the book, then try to say what was in it. Reading it again feels easier, but trying to remember without looking is what makes it stick. It's like practising a football match, not watching one.

**The real words:** Answering from memory instead of re-reading; the best-evidenced study technique; what the contract's quizzes and explain-backs force. → [02 Piece 1](../modules/02-tutor-setup/TEACH.md)

**See it:** `C:\Users\samra\repos\ai-mastery\modules\01-video-canon\exercises\ex02-close-window-retype.md` — read the code, close the window, type it back.

### Dojo
**Like you're 5:** A daily treasure hunt. Someone hides three mistakes in a short piece of writing, and you have twenty minutes to find them, one guess at a time, saying why for each.

**The real words:** The daily 20-minute planted-bug hunt run with the root-cause method; levels L0–L4. → [02 dojo.md](../modules/02-tutor-setup/dojo.md)

**See it:** `C:\Users\samra\repos\ai-mastery\modules\02-tutor-setup\dojo.md` — the minute-by-minute table and the five levels.

### FSRS
**Like you're 5:** A clever alarm clock inside Anki. It watches which cards you forget and when, learns how fast YOU forget, and rings each card back just before it would slip away.

**The real words:** Anki's built-in modern scheduler; learns your forgetting curve and sets each card's next review (your numbers: retention 0.90, 10 new a day). → [02 Piece 2](../modules/02-tutor-setup/TEACH.md)

**See it:** In Anki: a deck's gear icon → Options → the FSRS section, with desired retention set to 0.90.

### Illusion of knowing
**Like you're 5:** Watching someone ride a bike makes you feel you could ride one too. Then you get on and fall over. Watching felt like learning; it wasn't.

**The real words:** Feeling you could do what you only watched being done; the failure the tutor contract exists to block. → [02 Piece 1](../modules/02-tutor-setup/TEACH.md)

**See it:** Read any solved exercise, close it, and open a blank file to write it yourself. The gap between "I followed that" and what you can type is the illusion.

### Spaced repetition
**Like you're 5:** Water a plant on the day the soil goes dry, not every day. Each time the plant is fine, you can wait a bit longer before the next drink. Cards work the same: each time you remember, the next review comes later.

**The real words:** Reviewing just before you'd forget, at stretching intervals (today, then 3 days, then 10, then a month). → [02 Piece 2](../modules/02-tutor-setup/TEACH.md)

**See it:** In Anki, the four answer buttons show the next interval above each one; press Good on a card you know and watch the interval grow.

### Tutor contract
**Like you're 5:** A deal you make with your helper before you start: "Don't do my homework for me. Ask me questions, show me a different example, mark my work hard, and make me fix it myself."

**The real words:** The 10-rule paste block that makes Claude a tutor, not a solver. → [02 tutor-contract.md](../modules/02-tutor-setup/tutor-contract.md)
